Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the games published under clustergames.io — including Scramble, Word Climb, WordCrush, and WordDrop — collect and how it's used. These are casual, single-player word games. There are no user accounts, no passwords, and no email or personal contact information collected.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous device identifier. On first launch, the app generates a random identifier and stores it on your device (in local storage). It isn't tied to your name, email, or any other real-world identity — it just lets the app recognize repeat visits from the same device, e.g. so resubmitting a score updates your existing leaderboard entry instead of creating a duplicate.
  • Display name (optional). If you choose to appear on a leaderboard, you can pick a display name. This name is shown publicly alongside your score to other players. Names are filtered for obvious profanity before being saved.
  • Gameplay statistics. Things like games played, words solved, scores, and win counts, used to power leaderboards and in-app achievements.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or any device permissions beyond what's needed to run the game.

How Information Is Used

  • To display leaderboards and personal achievements within the games.
  • To keep your progress and preferences (like sound settings) consistent between sessions.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Where Information Is Stored

Leaderboard and stats data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Data that never leaves your device (like your mute preference) is stored only in your browser or app's local storage.

Your Choices

  • You can play every game fully without ever setting a display name or appearing on a leaderboard.
  • You can clear your local device identifier and preferences at any time by clearing your browser's site data (web) or deleting/reinstalling the app (iOS).
  • To request removal of a leaderboard entry or report an inappropriate display name, contact us — see below.

Children's Privacy

These games are suitable for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the games evolve. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date above.

Contact Us

Questions, privacy requests, or content to report? Email support@clustergames.io.
